Keypad basics

  • Arm: enter your code, then press Away or Home.
  • Disarm: enter your code, then press Disarm.
  • X cancels a code you have started typing.
  • ✓ bypasses open sensors when the keypad says “Sensors require bypass.”
  • Panic: hold X and ✓ together for 3 seconds. On a phone use two fingers. On a computer hold Esc and Enter.
  • Police and Fire: hold the button for 3 seconds. The siren sounds and your emergency contacts are notified.
  • Medical: hold the button for 3 seconds. An alert is sent and the siren stays quiet.
  • Light bar: shows how much time is left during the exit and entry delays.

Practice settings

Any 4 digits. Everyone you share this with can set their own.

Keyboard: 0–9, Esc or Backspace for X, Enter for ✓, A for Away, H for Home, D for Disarm. Emergency buttons: hold with a mouse or finger, or hold Space or Enter while one is focused.

Unofficial practice tool, not made by or connected to Ring. Delays here are short so practice moves quickly (8 seconds to exit, 15 to enter). A real system lets you set both from 0 to 180 seconds, and your setup may differ from this one.
